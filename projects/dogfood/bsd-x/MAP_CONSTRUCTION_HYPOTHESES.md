# Two independently proposed map constructions

No web search was used. This document separates algebraic formulas we can
construct from the missing arithmetic assertions. No actual comparison from
the Selmer space to analytic data is constructed or certified here.

## First recipe: obtain more observations from a logarithmic functional

Suppose a Q_p-linear functional ell:S_p->Q_p has been constructed. Consider
z -> (F_1(ell(z)),...,F_b(ell(z))) for polynomials F_i. This is a natural
candidate for manufacturing additional coordinates from one observation.

It cannot distinguish z from z+k when ell(k)=0, for ANY functions F_i.
Consequently all such postprocessing retains the original hidden directions.
Its Jacobian rows, wherever the derivatives exist, are multiples of ell.
Composing with scalar endomorphisms z -> n*z has the same limitation.

Moreover, for F(t)=sum_(j=1)^d a_j t^j over a characteristic-zero field,
with a_d!=0 and d>=2, the coefficient of x^(d-1)y in

    F(x+y)-F(x)-F(y)

is d*a_d!=0. Thus only polynomials of degree at most one, with zero
constant, give additive maps. Squaring an observation supplies a nonlinear
coordinate, not another independent linear Selmer functional.

RAD computes the offending coefficient symbolically for each polynomial,
not just at a few chosen x,y. Its diagnostic functional is ell(z)=z1+z2+z3,
whose hidden direction (1,-1,0) persists for every postprocessing recipe.
The generic_differential_rank field is the generic Jacobian rank; it is not
misidentified as the linear rank of a nonlinear map.

## Second recipe: construct observations by pairing with rational points

Assume X1 has supplied m independent points P_1,...,P_m. Let S_p be the
rational Selmer space. PROPOSE constructing a symmetric Q_p-bilinear pairing
H on the FULL space S_p, and define the explicit candidate formula

    Phi_H(z)=(H(z,P_1),...,H(z,P_m)).

This is linear if H exists with that domain. The program does not construct
such an arithmetic pairing. A real Neron-Tate height cannot simply be
declared to have Q_p coefficients or to extend to all Selmer directions.

Suppose also that B=(H(P_i,P_j)) is invertible. Write W=span(P_1,...,P_m).
Then for any z in S_p the formula

    k(z) = z - sum_i P_i [B^(-1) Phi_H(z)]_i

constructs a vector in K=ker(Phi_H). Every z decomposes uniquely into a
vector of W and a vector of K, so

    S_p = W direct-sum K.

This is a useful explicit construction of the missing direction, not a proof
that K=0. It also shows exactly where a kernel-exclusion argument must act.

In the three-dimensional diagnostic with m=2, take

    H = [[1,0,u], [0,1,v], [u,v,w]].

The candidate map consists of its first two rows. RAD constructs

    k=(-u,-v,1),   H*k=(0,0,w-u^2-v^2).

The scalar w-u^2-v^2 is the Schur complement. If it is nonzero, the full
pairing is nondegenerate but the candidate map STILL has the nonzero kernel
vector k. If it is zero, k lies in the radical of the full pairing as well.
Both branches must be distinguished: nondegeneracy of a pairing does not
make its restriction to m observations injective on a larger space.
Passing to S_p/K would make the map injective, but would change the required
domain and would not establish the bound on dim(S_p).

## A precise new arithmetic hypothesis to investigate

For the pairing route, after constructing H and establishing invertibility
of B, the remaining hypothesis can be stated as follows:

    If ord_(s=1) L(E,s)>=d, and there are d independent rational points
    with invertible H-Gram matrix, then a nonzero Selmer vector orthogonal
    to all d points forces L^(d)(E,1)=0.

This is an independently proposed arithmetic vanishing statement, NOT a
deduction from the matrix identities and NOT claimed proved. At d=m, the
derivative is nonzero by definition of analytic rank, so this statement
would imply K=0. The candidate map would then give dim S_p=m and X3.
This route is conditional on X1 as well as the pairing and vanishing inputs.

The hypothesis explicitly asks for a link between an orthogonal Selmer
class and an additional zero of the complex L-function. That link cannot
be generated by renaming the kernel, assuming the vanishing implication,
or asserting that synthetic pairing coefficients are arithmetic heights.
The experiment specifies a proposed mechanism and the exact theorem it
would need; it has not filled any universal X.
